1. Who we are

Quick Notes is a personal notes app made by Brad Lipetzky. It is a private app for Brad, his family, and the people they invite to share notes — not a public product or a commercial service.

If you have questions about this policy or about your data, email blipetzky@gmail.com.

2. What Quick Notes is

Quick Notes is available for Windows and for Android (package name com.forge.quicknotes). You can create notes, organize them with folders and tags, leave comments, move items to the trash, add pictures and files, and keep everything in sync across your devices.

This website is a static home page and privacy policy. It does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not collect information about visitors.

3. Information we handle

Account and sign-in

To sync across devices, you sign in with your email address. Quick Notes sends a one-time code to that address. We do not use a password. Your email address is stored so the app can recognize your account and send sign-in codes.

Notes and related content

The app stores the content you create or add, which may include:

  • Note titles and body text
  • Folders, tags, colors, pins, comments, and reminders
  • Items in the trash, until you empty it
  • Pictures and other files you attach to a note

Notes, folders, tags, comments, and trash sync through a private Supabase project. Access is controlled with per-user rules, so each person can see only their own notes and notes that have been shared with them.

4. Google Drive and Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you choose to connect it, Quick Notes uses Google APIs for two purposes only:

  • Pictures and attachments. When you add a picture or file to a note, Quick Notes stores it in a folder named “Quick Notes” in your Google Drive. The default permission is the drive.file scope, which lets the app access only files it creates itself — not the rest of your Drive.
  • Google Doc copies. If you choose to share a note as a Google Doc, the app creates that document in your Drive on your request.

Optionally, you can grant a broader Google Drive permission (the drive scope) so Quick Notes can display pictures in notes that other people have shared with you. This is off unless you turn it on, and it is used only to show those shared pictures in the app.

Quick Notes does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, credit decisions, or any purpose other than providing the features described above. Google user data is not sold or transferred to third parties except as needed to run the app (for example, storing a file in your own Drive) or as required by law.

5. Limited Use disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy Quick Notes's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. What we don’t do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not use analytics or other tracking tools in the app or on this website.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with others for their own purposes.
  • We do not use your notes, files, or Google user data for anything other than providing Quick Notes.

7. Sharing and service providers

You can share a note with someone you choose inside the app. A Google Doc copy is created only when you ask for one.

To run the app, Quick Notes uses:

  • Supabase, to store and sync account and note data under per-user access rules.
  • Google, if you connect Drive, to store pictures and attachments you add and to create Google Doc copies you request.

Sign-in codes are emailed to the address you enter. That mail is not used for marketing.

These providers process data only to provide their services to the app. We do not otherwise disclose your information except if required by law, or if needed to protect the rights, safety, or security of the people who use Quick Notes.

8. Your choices

  • You can edit or delete notes in the app, including items in the trash.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive from Quick Notes at any time.
  • You can review and revoke Quick Notes’ access to your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can email blipetzky@gmail.com to ask a question, or to request that account data stored for sync be deleted.

9. Security

Sign-in uses a one-time email code rather than a reusable password. Note data is protected with per-user access rules, and traffic to the sync service and to Google uses encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but these steps are in place to keep your information limited to the people who should see it.

10. Children

Quick Notes is a personal family app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above. For meaningful changes, we will also try to let signed-in users know in the app or by email.
