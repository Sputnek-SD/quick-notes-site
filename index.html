Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

Built for everyday notes at home — and designed so your words stay yours.

Sync in seconds

Sign in with a code emailed to you. Notes, folders, tags, comments, and trash stay in sync, with access rules so each person only sees what they’re meant to see.

Folders, tags, and color

Organize the way you already think. Pin what matters, leave a comment, move something to the trash, and find it again with search.

Pictures in your Drive

Photos and files you add to a note live in a Quick Notes folder in your own Google Drive. By default, the app can only touch files it creates.

Share as a Google Doc

When you want to send a note outside the app, Quick Notes can make a Google Doc copy — only when you ask it to.

Windows and Android

The same app on a PC and a phone, including foldables and tablets, so a list you start in the kitchen is waiting on your desk.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of your data. What’s in your notes is used only to provide the app.

Your data stays in your accounts.

  • Notes sync through a private project with per-user access rules.
  • Google Drive is used only for pictures, attachments, and Docs you choose to create.
  • You can disconnect Google at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • This website is a static page. It does not use cookies, ads, or analytics.
